# Content, voice and compliance rules

## Voice
- Informational-authoritative, beginner-first, calm. The "decision problem, not superstition" voice outranks "Reel in 5000x JACKPOT!!" on YMYL pages.
- Second person ("you"), light "we tested / we checked".
- Class 8–9 English (Indian ESL, mobile skim). 12–18 words per sentence. One idea per sentence.
- Rupees everywhere (₹10 min bet, ₹300-type promo minimums), UPI / Paytm / PhonePe / Google Pay named, "in India" 4–6 times naturally per long page.
- Freshness signals: "today", current year, visible "Updated <Month Year>".
- Open every page with a short plain/casual paragraph (~8% of the page), then professional tone.
- Paragraph-first. Points + tables + callouts ≤ 30% of the page. Do not stack design blocks; vary them (spec grid, callout, do/don't, steps, table, cards) across the page.
- No em dashes. Use commas, colons or full stops.
- No "learn", "discover", "explained" in meta descriptions; description 158 chars, keyword first, year included, varied wording per page.

## Honesty is the ranking edge (say these plainly)
- History is not prediction. Trackers show past spins only. If a bonus has not appeared for a while it is not "due".
- A live game show usually has no true demo; watch mode / low stakes / observation are the alternatives.
- No APK is needed; fake APKs and "predictor" apps steal data or push unsafe casinos.
- Leaf/base-bet RTP vs bonus-bet RTP are different; show both and the house edge.
- A real game can be hosted by a poor platform: check provider AND operator (licence, KYC, withdrawal terms).
- Max win is rare; never frame it as an expected result.

## Never write
- Guaranteed wins, "trick / hack / pattern that works", signal groups, Telegram tips, prediction tools or any sell of them.
- "Provably fair", server seed / client seed on an Evolution wheel game.
- Hard bonus figures as headline promises or "Win Real Money" H1s; keep promo copy factual with "T&Cs apply, 18+".
- Operator login pages as SEO targets; brand terms of competitors as page focus (light contextual mention max).
- Author real names, agency/company names, personal emails. Use "Editorial Team" and a role-based contact form/email on the contact page.

## Always include
- 18+ age gate; "18+ · Play responsibly" line with KIRAN helpline 1800-599-0019 (24x7) in gate, footer and the Responsible Gaming section.
- Responsible-gambling callout (`call-rg`) near the end of every content page; full /responsible-gambling/ page in plain prose (no design blocks).
- Tax note on real-money pages: flat 30% on net winnings under Section 115BBJ, TDS under 194BA, no loss offset.
- Legal framing: state-wise variation in India, Public Gambling Act 1867, Promotion and Regulation of Online Gaming Act 2025; site is informational and does not operate gambling services.
- Footer affiliate disclaimer + "informational content only" line.
- Author box: "Reviewed by the Editorial Team", method sentence, Updated date.
- Evolution Gaming (or Galaxsys) attribution in the first paragraph. Google expects provider entity.

## LOCKED-DATA discipline
Create one fact table per game (segments, probabilities, base and max multipliers, RTP per bet, min bet, round time, release date, platforms) from the provider's official page before writing. Every page quotes the same figures. Where sources disagree (e.g. 53 segments / 97.10% official vs 56 / 97.44% stats sites) pick the official figure and stay consistent site-wide.

## FAQ
12 questions on the homepage, 5–8 per inner page, grouped under H2s on /faqs/. Wording of on-page `summary` must equal FAQPage schema `name`; answer text must equal `acceptedAnswer`. Mix English and Hinglish phrasing only on the Hindi pages.

## Hindi / regional pages
Hinglish with Devanagari connective words and English game terms (Leaf, Lil' Blues, RTP, UPI). Same H2 order as the English page it mirrors. `lang="hi-IN"`, body class `l-hi`, hreflang pair with the English page, Hindi nav labels only for the language switch.
